Official token information

Mutt Rewards (MRWD)

MRWD is a utility and liquidity-incentive token on Base. It rewards liquidity providers, enables staking-based access to Equilnyxs Pro, and supports rewards for sponsored operations in the Shaggy Mutt DEX ecosystem.

Identity notice: Mutt Rewards is plural, its symbol is MRWD, and its only canonical Base token contract is 0xC17227AAa33C54634B6cE7AD0bf33A156563c179. Tokens with similar names or the same symbol on other networks are unrelated.

What MRWD is used for

Liquidity rewards

MRWD rewards eligible liquidity providers in the canonical MRWD/WETH Uniswap V3 incentive.

Equilnyxs Pro access

Users can lock MRWD in the dedicated staking contract to qualify for Equilnyxs Pro access.

Sponsored-operation rewards

Eligible successful operations sponsored by the ecosystem paymaster can reward users in MRWD.

Launchpad quote asset

MRWD is designed to serve as an optional quote asset for ecosystem token launches and routed trades.
