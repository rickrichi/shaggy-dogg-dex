Mutt Rewards (MRWD)

MRWD is a fixed-supply utility and liquidity-incentive token on Base. It rewards liquidity providers across a 16-quarter (600B MRWD) schedule, unlocks Equilnyxs Pro via 30-day principal-protected staking, and supports sponsored-operation rewards in the Shaggy Mutt DEX ecosystem.

What MRWD is used for

Liquidity rewards

Equilnyxs Pro access

Users can lock MRWD in the dedicated staking contract to qualify for Equilnyxs Pro access.

Sponsored-operation rewards

Eligible successful operations sponsored by the ecosystem paymaster can reward users in MRWD.

Launchpad quote asset

MRWD is designed to serve as an optional quote asset for ecosystem token launches and routed trades.
