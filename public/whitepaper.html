Abstract

Shaggy Dogg DEX is a wallet-connected application for trading tokens, managing decentralized liquidity, launching fixed-supply tokens and DeFi suites, and operating ecosystem contracts. Its trading interface compares supported on-chain routes, filters unsafe outlier quotes, and submits user-authorized transactions to the selected exchange router. Base is the home network; the application also configures swap routes on Ethereum, Arbitrum One, Optimism, Polygon, and Sepolia.

This whitepaper documents the current live state of the application and Mutt Rewards ($MRWD), an immutable fixed-supply utility and liquidity-incentive token on Base whose core functions are rewarding liquidity provision in its canonical Uniswap V3 pool, unlocking Equilnyxs Pro through MuttProStaking, rewarding eligible sponsored operations in SponsoredRewardPaymaster, and serving as a planned optional quote asset for launchpad tokens. Part III separates three kinds of claims, because only the first can be guaranteed by code:

  • Cryptographically enforced: properties the contracts make impossible to violate.
  • Economically incentivized: outcomes the design rewards but cannot force.
  • Market-dependent: outcomes that depend on participants the project does not control.

Part I — The trading system

1. Purpose and scope

Decentralized trading requires users to identify the correct token, compare pools, approve a spender, execute a swap, and monitor the resulting position. Shaggy Dogg DEX brings trading, liquidity management, token deployment, staking, yield vaults, protocol operations, and arbitrage workflows into one application while keeping settlement on the selected blockchain. It does not introduce a consensus network or bridge assets between chains; a token on Base is distinct from a similarly named token elsewhere.

2. System architecture

The application has four principal layers.

  1. Wallet and interface. React, TypeScript, and Vite provide the frontend; Wagmi, Viem, and RainbowKit connect wallets and construct contract reads and writes. Fonts (Inter and JetBrains Mono) are bundled locally so pages make no external font requests. Ordinary trading, liquidity, staking, and launchpad flows ask the user's wallet to sign every transaction.
  2. Quotation and data. Public and configured RPC clients read on-chain pools, positions, and allowances. The Hono backend and Vercel routes serve market data, arbitrage spread snapshots, launch verification, and paymaster policy checks. Displayed prices and analytics inform the interface; transaction receipts are the evidence of execution.
  3. Execution. Exchange routers execute swaps; position managers and liquidity contracts hold LP positions; project contracts implement fixed-supply tokens, atomic suite deployment, fee-discount staking, Aave and Compound vault accounting, V3 position timelocks, MRWD incentive scheduling, vesting, Pro staking, and flash-loan routing.
  4. Operations. Contract administrators configure parameters only where the contract permits it. A dedicated GitHub Actions keeper wallet triggers permissionless MuttIncentiveScheduler periods, and operator tools run arbitrage checks. Their permissions and custody boundaries differ from the wallet-connected interface.

3.2 Candidate selection, outlier filtering, and venue choice

The route engine considers a direct pair and, where applicable, a two-hop path through the chain's wrapped native asset (WETH) or USDC. V3 candidates are direct pools across configured fee tiers and, for a two-hop path, fee-tier pairs executed with the router's multi-hop exactInput (with SushiSwap V3 using its original SwapRouter deadline-in-struct and address(0) recipient convention before unwrapWETH9). Quotes are collected at a shared block, failed or zero quotes are excluded, and each venue's price impact is measured against that venue's own factory and pools.

Cross-venue outlier quote filter (admitVenueQuotes). An initialized V3 pool with negligible liquidity at an off-market tick can return a numerically valid quote without reverting. When at least three independent venues return positive quotes for a trade, the router computes the cross-venue median of each venue's best output and excludes any venue whose best quote deviates from that median by more than 35% (MAX_QUOTE_DEVIATION_BPS = 3,500) before selecting or displaying routes.

By default (Best price (automatic)), the highest quoted output among admitted candidates is selected. Users can also select a specific venue from the Route selector or the Quotes for your trade cards:

  • A user-selected venue drives the quote, price impact, router approval, and swap call (Swap on <venue>).
  • The interface shows how a chosen venue compares with the best quote and highlights choices that are 1% or worse below the best quote.
  • If a chosen venue has no route after a pair or amount change, the swap is blocked and Switch to best price is offered; the application never silently reroutes a user-chosen venue.

The router does not split orders across pools or rank by gas-adjusted proceeds.

3.3 Transaction protection and non-standard approvals

For quoted output Q and slippage tolerance s in basis points:

minimumOutput = floor(Q × (10,000 − s) / 10,000)

Quotes older than 30 seconds are treated as stale. Before submission the interface re-reads the route, checks price impact, and simulates the transaction; approvals use the exact trade or deposit amount; the deadline buffer is 15 minutes.

Some tokens (such as USDT on Ethereum) revert when approve changes an existing non-zero allowance to another non-zero value. When a wallet already has a non-zero allowance smaller than the required amount, approvalSequence() simulates the direct approve(spender, needed) call first and, only if the token rejects it, prepends approve(spender, 0) before setting the new allowance. Standard ERC-20 tokens still require only a single approval. Sources: [R1], [R2].

4. Price data and Market Data

Prices come from the self-hosted market-data service: exchange order-book medians for listed assets, and Base on-chain pool reserves for tokens no exchange lists. No third-party price API is used. /market-data opens on AERO by default.

  • On-chain venues read. The service reads Base Uniswap V2 pairs, Uniswap V3 pools (0.05%, 0.30%, and 1.00% tiers, priced from slot0 and valued for liquidity depth at twice the pool's WETH or USDC reserve), and Aerodrome volatile pools. It does not read Aerodrome concentrated-liquidity (Slipstream) pools.
  • Liquidity floor. A pool price is treated as firm only when the pool holds at least MD_MIN_POOL_LIQUIDITY_USD (default $100). Below that floor (down to MD_MIN_INDICATIVE_LIQUIDITY_USD, default $1), the price is returned only when requested with allowThin=true and is flagged indicative alongside the pool's USD liquidity, because a thin pool's price is cheap to move. Indicative prices are never written into price history.
  • Portfolio accounting. Held tokens whose pools sit below the $100 floor (such as SD and FBA at current pool sizes) display their indicative price and pool depth on /portfolio and /market-data with sub-cent significant digits preserved, but indicative prices are excluded from the portfolio total, 24-hour change, and largest-holding summary. Following the November 2026 liquidity additions (§13.2–§13.3), the canonical MRWD/WETH 0.30% V3 pool holds 0.0463 WETH (~$107.27 single-sided WETH, ~$214.54 total pool depth at 2 × WETH), clearing the $100 firm-liquidity floor.
  • Honest failure. When the service cannot price an asset, the API returns 503 rather than a fabricated or stale value, and the interface reports that price data is unavailable. Sources: [R10], [R11].

5. Arbitrage tooling

Arbitrage tools compare execution opportunities across decentralized and centralized venues. A displayed spread is an estimate; gas, pool fees, flash-loan premiums, slippage, and transaction ordering can eliminate it.

  • Public spread snapshot (/arbitrage). On the public website, /arbitrage polls /api/arbitrage/snapshot from the self-hosted market-data spread feed to display live cross-venue opportunities without requiring a local bot process; when a local bot engine is explicitly configured via VITE_BOT_API_URL, operator controls connect to that engine.
  • CeFi Pair Matrix (/arbitrage/matrix). Connects directly from the browser to live WebSocket order-book and ticker streams from Coinbase, Kraken, Binance (wss://data-stream.binance.vision), Bybit (wss://stream.bybit.com), and OKX (wss://ws.okx.com:8443) to compare centralized spreads against Base DEX quotes.
  • Browser Auto-Pilot. Each tick reads live quotes from Base Uniswap V3 and Aerodrome pools, scans multiple candidate intermediate assets (including USDC and cbBTC) for triangular routes, and reads Aerodrome's on-chain pool fee and Aave's on-chain flash-loan premium. If any leg fails to quote, the tick is skipped.
  • Execution and profit guard. Wallet-funded triangular trades execute via SwapRouter02 exactInput on the quoted path. Flash-loan routes execute through the pinned AtomicFlashArbitrageRouter (0xdF77eBFB6e0468aE4e94b13Fc9881427B82178Fd, executeGenericArbitrage) inside a single Aave V3 flash loan so every leg settles atomically or the transaction reverts. Screening and pre-signature checks price L2 execution gas plus the L1 data fee and require quoted net profit to cover both gas and the user's profit target.

Every dispatch is simulated before signing. These controls make losing on-chain execution revert rather than settle; they do not guarantee profitable opportunities. Sources: [R9], [R12].

Part II — Liquidity, launchpad, custody, and contracts

6. Liquidity, position ownership, and launchpad

6.1 V2 pools, V3 positions, and DEX liquidity explorer

V2 pools represent proportional reserve ownership with fungible LP tokens (x × y = k). Deposits must respect the pool ratio, and the initial deposit sets the starting price. When no wallet is connected, /liquidity defaults to Base (8453); on Ethereum (1), /liquidity opens directly on classic Uniswap V2 while Base-only V2→V3 migration, V3 position manager, and V3 timelock tools offer a one-click switch to Base.

V3 positions are NFTs with a fee tier and price range. A position adds depth and earns fees only while the market price stays inside its range. On Base, /liquidity discovers every V3 position a wallet holds, stakes in UniswapV3Staker, or locks in V3PositionTimelock.

DEX liquidity explorer (/stake/dexes). Compares seven active Base liquidity venues (Uniswap V3, Uniswap V2, Aerodrome, PancakeSwap V3, SushiSwap V3, PancakeSwap V2, and SushiSwap V2; BaseSwap is used for swap routing in §3.1 but omitted from the liquidity directory because its web frontend is parked), discovers held and staked tokens across ecosystem contracts (even when wallet balance is zero because the balance is staked), and provides guided in-app pool planners (POOL_PLANNERS) and pair-specific deposit links.

Sovereign V3 position timelock. V3PositionTimelock (0xb5c9bedb57f4e348e40a5e6326c90de474072e25) holds a V3 position NFT until a user-chosen unlock timestamp. The recorded lock owner can collect fees (collectFees), extend the lock (extendLock), and withdraw the NFT after expiry (withdraw). The contract has no administrator override and no early withdrawal path. Positions must enter through lockPosition. Sources: [R3], [R4].

6.2 Token launchpad and atomic suite deployment

The launchpad (/launchpad) is open to any wallet on Base mainnet. Wizard steps (/launchpad/identity, /launchpad/purpose, /launchpad/liquidity, /launchpad/review) redirect incomplete token identities back to Step 1 while persisting drafts across reloads in sessionStorage. Key launchpad mechanics enforced in source:

  • Fixed-supply token (FixedSupplyToken.sol). Mints 100% of supply once in the constructor to the recipient wallet, with 18 decimals and no mint, owner, admin, pause, fee, or blacklist. Its _transfer function reverts on to == address(this) ("Transfer to token contract") so tokens cannot be accidentally stranded in the ownerless token contract. The server's launch verifier matches both approved FixedSupplyToken creation builds (0x692f…9343 and 0x79c0…1662).
  • On-chain launch fee (LaunchpadFees). Before deploying any contract, the launchpad reads live feeAmount() from 0x2443C90eB4eA63b74383a5f0dB36c95A1091b68b (10 USDC, 10_000_000 base units) and verifies the connected wallet holds enough USDC. After the token deploys, the user approves and calls payLaunchFee(tokenAddress).
  • Atomic suite deployment (LaunchpadSuiteDeployer.sol). For the Staking, Aave Vault, and Full Suite cards, a single LaunchpadSuiteDeployer transaction deploys and configures FeeDiscountStaking (lock, unstake fee, and four discount tiers) and any selected vaults and router, verifies that USDC (0x8335…2913), Aave v3 Pool (0xA238…d1c5), aUSDC (0x4e65…c0AB), and Compound v3 USDC Comet (0xb125…Eb2F) match their canonical Base addresses, revokes its own temporary operator/admin privileges, and transfers every admin and tierAdmin role to the creator before returning. A launch can never end half-configured.
  • Multi-DEX liquidity seeding. Optional initial liquidity seeding (minimum 0.05 ETH) supports Base Uniswap V3 (1.00% tier, full-range position), Uniswap V2, PancakeSwap V2, and Aerodrome volatile pools (stable = false).
  • In-app staking and contract management. Token holders can stake and unstake launched tokens on /stake (including via shareable /stake?contract=0x... links, Explore Launches, and My Tokens); LaunchStakingPanel verifies that the contract's runtime bytecode matches the pinned FeeDiscountStaking build byte-for-byte (outside its immutable token slot) before acting. Creators can withdraw accrued staking fees, pause/resume or recover vaults, and manage flash routers from /launchpad/my-tokens, or load any approved contract on Load my contract (which checks approved runtime bytecode hashes in contract-versions.json before checking admin). Sources: [R2], [R6], [R7], [R13].

7. Fee-discount staking and lending vaults

7.1 Fee-discount staking (FeeDiscountStaking.sol)

FeeDiscountStaking accepts its immutable configured token and records balances by account. Its implementation enforces the following rules:

  • Capped lock and per-stake unlock snapshot. setMinimumStakePeriod is hard-capped at MAXIMUM_STAKE_PERIOD = 365 days. When a user stakes, unlockTimestamp[msg.sender] = block.timestamp + minimumStakePeriod snapshots the unlock time for that position, so a later admin increase to minimumStakePeriod affects future stakes only and cannot extend existing locks. Adding stake to an existing position resets the lock for the user's whole staked balance.
  • Unstake fee and isolated fee accounting. setWithdrawalFee is capped at 5,000 bps (50%) and applies at withdrawal. Withheld unstake fees accumulate in accruedFees, and withdrawFees(to, amount) allows the administrator to withdraw at most accruedFees, keeping staked principal unreachable by the administrator.
  • Vault discount tiers. Launchpad suites configure four ascending tiers based on supply share: staking 0.01%, 0.1%, 1%, and 10% of total supply grants 10%, 25%, 50%, and 75% discounts (1,000 / 2,500 / 5,000 / 7,500 bps) on linked vault performance fees. Staking alone pays no yield.

For the SD token (0x051c758521971f5c5a84edaf18eea12854375469), the active Base FeeDiscountStaking contract is 0x8a765d450d38d13f5e2533d29399f743cdf23faa. Sources: [R6], [R13].

7.2 Lending vaults (YieldStakingVault.sol and CompoundYieldVault.sol)

YieldStakingVault (Aave v3) and CompoundYieldVault (Compound v3 Comet) accept USDC on Base, track internal shares and principal per depositor, and supply deposits to their lending market. With assets A before deposit, total shares S, deposit d, and virtual offsets (VIRTUAL_SHARES = 1,000,000, VIRTUAL_ASSETS = 1):

sharesMinted     = floor(d × (S + 1,000,000) / (A + 1))
grossWithdrawal  = floor(sharesRedeemed × (A + 1) / (S + 1,000,000))
effectiveFeeBps  = floor(baseFeeBps × (10,000 − discountBps) / 10,000)
performanceFee   = floor(positiveYield × effectiveFeeBps / 10,000)

Both vault contracts enforce identical trust boundaries:

  • Immutable market and staking wiring. underlyingAsset, discountStaking, and the lending market addresses (aavePool and aToken in YieldStakingVault; comet in CompoundYieldVault) are immutable. Neither vault has a market-repointing setter, so an administrator cannot redirect deposited funds to a fake market.
  • No fee increase while shares exist. performanceFeeBps defaults to 10% (1,000 bps) and is capped at 30% (3,000 bps). setPerformanceFee requires totalShares == 0 || newFeeBps <= performanceFeeBps, so the administrator can lower the fee at any time but cannot raise it while any depositor holds shares.
  • Pause and vault-only emergency recovery. setPaused(true) blocks new deposit calls while leaving withdraw open. While paused, emergencyWithdrawFromAave / emergencyWithdrawFromComet pulls funds from the immutable lending market only back into the vault contract (address(this)) so depositors can withdraw their shares; the administrator cannot withdraw depositor principal. Source: [R7].

8. Sponsored Reward Paymaster and administrative authority

8.1 Sponsored Reward Paymaster (SponsoredRewardPaymaster.sol)

SponsoredRewardPaymaster is deployed on Base mainnet at 0x9cD40EBa4B0793C43FdF8C5A3272073820f77CB4 on ERC-4337 EntryPoint v0.7 (0x0000000071727De22E5E9d8BAf0edAc6f37da032). It sponsors gas in ETH from its EntryPoint deposit for eligible Base ERC-20 transfers, swaps (on allowlisted Uniswap V3, Uniswap V2, and Aerodrome routers), and staking calls (MuttProStaking and launchpad FeeDiscountStaking), and pays 100 MRWD to the user inside _postOp when mode == PostOpMode.opSucceeded. Each sponsorship is capped at 0.0001 ETH of gas with a 24-hour per-wallet cooldown.

Current browser-wallet status: gas-free operations are disabled in the UI. While the contract path is verified on a Base fork with real EIP-7702 delegated accounts and EntryPoint v0.7 (SponsoredRewardPaymaster.t.sol), current browser wallets cannot submit compatible signatures:

  1. MetaMask EIP-7702 Smart Account (0x63c0…E32B, EntryPoint v0.7): MetaMask refuses website EIP-712 UserOperation signature requests whose verifyingContract is the internal account itself.
  2. Plain browser (JSON-RPC) wallets: cannot sign an EIP-7702 authorization for a website.
  3. Simple 7702 accounts (0xe6Ca…555B, EntryPoint v0.8): use EntryPoint v0.8, whereas the reward paymaster answers only EntryPoint v0.7; the API refuses to sign mismatched EntryPoint requests.

Accordingly, /paymaster explains why sponsorship is unavailable for the connected wallet and disables the sponsored send button until wallet-native ERC-7677 wallet_sendCalls support is available. Source: [R14].

For project-deployed contracts that have an administrator role, that role is held by the single-key wallet 0x9dC0a2E92cC1f08050D2c5F560A45229CcF47C98. For launchpad suites deployed by users, LaunchpadSuiteDeployer transfers all administrator roles to the creator's recipient wallet.

9. Security model and limitations

  • Trading and liquidity: thin pools, adverse ordering, token restrictions, and changing prices can materially affect execution outcomes.
  • Contract custody: deposited or staked assets follow each contract's rules; wallet custody before a deposit does not mean immediate withdrawal after it.
  • Administration: for contracts with admin roles, a compromised admin key or adverse parameter change (such as raising withdrawalFeeBps up to 50% in FeeDiscountStaking) can affect users.
  • Dependencies: RPC providers, wallet extensions, external lending markets (Aave v3, Compound v3), centralized exchange WebSockets, and the market-data service can fail or lag.
  • Bytecode verification: the application's contract loader checks runtime bytecode hashes (contract-versions.json) before interacting with user-supplied staking, vault, or router contracts and refuses unapproved contracts.

The repository includes unit tests and Base-fork test suites (YieldVaultBaseFork.t.sol, CompoundVaultBaseFork.t.sol, LaunchpadSuiteBaseFork.t.sol, FlashRouterBaseFork.t.sol, MuttIncentiveScheduler.t.sol, SponsoredRewardPaymaster.t.sol, and V3PositionTimelock.t.sol) that run against real Base USDC, Aave v3, Compound v3, Uniswap V2/V3, Aerodrome, UniswapV3Staker, and EntryPoint v0.7, alongside a self-assessment in audit/KNOWN-ISSUES.md. Passing tests and Sourcify/Blockscout verification do not constitute an independent security audit, and this paper asserts no completed external audit. Report vulnerabilities privately via private vulnerability reporting or the security policy. No bug bounty is offered.

Part III — Mutt Rewards ($MRWD), the liquidity-rewards and utility token

11. Purpose and design principle

Mutt Rewards ($MRWD) (0xC17227AAa33C54634B6cE7AD0bf33A156563c179 on Base mainnet, canonical page dex.lumsian.com/mrwd) is a utility and liquidity-incentive token. It is not a meme token, and it is not a claim on project revenue, profit, treasury assets, or governance. Public listings and search profiles must use only its canonical Base address above.

MRWD has four documented functions in the ecosystem:

  1. Liquidity-provider rewards (live): rewarding eligible liquidity providers who stake in-range positions from the canonical MRWD/WETH 0.30% Uniswap V3 pool in UniswapV3Staker (§14).
  2. Equilnyxs Pro access (live): locking 1 billion MRWD in MuttProStaking to unlock Equilnyxs Pro without paying a subscription fee (§11.1).
  3. Sponsored-operation rewards (deployed on-chain): paying 100 MRWD per eligible sponsored operation in SponsoredRewardPaymaster (§8.1).
  4. Launchpad quote asset (planned): serving as an optional quote asset for tokens launched on the launchpad once the launchpad demand test (§17.2) is evaluated.

The design starts from a fundamental economic question: why would anyone hold or acquire the token after liquidity incentives decline? A rewards program can rent liquidity temporarily without creating lasting demand. Reward sustainability and durable demand are therefore treated as explicit design requirements (§16–§17), without adding transfer taxes, governance promises, or revenue sharing.

11.1 Demand architecture and Equilnyxs Pro staking

Primary (planned): MRWD-quoted launches. The launchpad is designed to gain an option to pair a new token against MRWD (PROJECT/MRWD), requiring creators who choose it to hold MRWD to seed the pool and routing WETH → MRWD → PROJECT trades through the canonical MRWD/WETH pool. Before enabling MRWD-quoted launches, actual launchpad activity is measured on WETH-paired launches during the three-month demand test window (§17.2).

Secondary (live): utility staking for Equilnyxs Pro. Equilnyxs, the project's language and toolchain for strategy code and Base DEX execution, sells Pro passes ($12 for 30 days, $99 for a year in USDC on Base). Staking MRWD is a live second path to Pro: keeping 1,000,000,000 (1 billion) MRWD locked in MuttProStaking unlocks a free Equilnyxs Pro key at https://www.lumsian.com/pro/stake.php, with staking managed on https://dex.lumsian.com/stake.

  • Staking contract (MuttProStaking, 0x67dBC193bF510225EBf189ab25dC3b34a32c1766). Separate from the token, non-upgradeable, and verified on Sourcify (exact_match), with no owner and no pause: each deposit has a fixed 30-day lock (lockSeconds = 2,592,000), there is no exit fee, matured deposits can always be withdrawn (withdrawMatured), and no function can move staked MRWD except back to its depositor. A wallet holds at most 32 open deposits. It records stakes only and pays no yield.
  • License issuance. The holder signs a message with the staking wallet. The store recovers the signer, reads the wallet's locked deposits, checks lockedUntil(wallet, T) against the 1 billion MRWD threshold, and issues an EQX1 Pro license valid until the day before the stake unlocks. A license therefore never outlives the stake behind it. Calling relockAll relocks all open deposits for a full 30-day period from block.timestamp.
  • Free features stay free. The Equilnyxs compiler, checker, REPL, dry runs, and test-network trading never require MRWD, and USDC passes remain available.

Target and model findings. The economic model (scripts/mchow-model/) puts the volume at which outside LPs stay without rewards at roughly $150,000–$220,000 per month through MRWD/WETH ($220,000/month conservative target, or ~$660/month in gross fees at 0.30%). The deployed 60/20/15/5 allocation passes every model criterion across all three organic scenarios at ~15 seeded launches per month ($40,000 first-month volume each), passes the base scenario at 5 launches per month ($25,000 each), and fails below 5 launches per month (§17.2). Sources: [R15], [R16].

13. Liquidity architecture and aggregator routing

13.1 Canonical pool and 56-aggregator routing audit

The canonical pool is the MRWD/WETH 0.30% Uniswap V3 pool on Base, 0xa7D4D975988DAa77B46c5d637717e2dFE07FbDB5, created through Uniswap's canonical Base V3 factory (0x33128a8fC17869897dcE68Ed026d694621f6FDfD) and position manager (0x03a520b32C04BF3bEEf7BEb72E919cf822Ed34f1). Because V3 liquidity is held as position NFTs with chosen price ranges, usable depth must be measured as active, in-range liquidity, not nominal deposited value. A second pool on another venue would split liquidity and would not share the UniswapV3Staker incentive key bound to this pool.

On /protocol, the 56-Aggregator Routing Audit (MrwdAggregatorAuditCard) quotes 11 on-chain Base routers and quoters live against the canonical MRWD/WETH V3 pool and classifies 56 aggregator routing targets (public/mrwd-56-aggregators/report.json), distinguishing venues that route through Uniswap V3 automatically from those requiring additional pools or off-chain indexing.

13.3 Pool price and valuation versus depth

Following the November 2026 full-range LP additions (staked positions #6367926, #6383531, #6383541, #6383553, #6448087, and #6448166), the canonical MRWD/WETH 0.30% V3 pool holds 0.04633571 WETH (~$107.27 single-sided WETH) and 171.44 billion MRWD (~$214.54 total pool depth at 2 × WETH), clearing the market-data service's $100 firm-liquidity floor (§4). 14. Rewards program

14.1 Mechanism: UniswapV3Staker and in-app claiming

Rewards are distributed through Uniswap's official UniswapV3Staker on Base, 0x42bE4D6527829FeFA1493e1fb9F3676d2425C3C1. Rewards accrue in proportion to a staked position's in-range liquidity multiplied by seconds in range:

reward ≈ unclaimedRewards × (Δ secondsPerLiquidityInside × positionLiquidity) / unclaimedLiquiditySeconds

Because secondsPerLiquidityInside is tracked by the V3 pool across all active in-range liquidity (staked or unstaked), any unstaked in-range position—including the locked launch position whenever the price is inside its range—dilutes the share of the period budget claimable by staked positions, and the unclaimed remainder goes to the burn-address refundee (0x…dEaD) when the period is finalized.

On /stake and /protocol, the MRWD rewards panel discovers the connected wallet's staked and unstaked MRWD/WETH V3 positions directly from contract state and provides three owner-checked actions:

  • Claim: unstakes the position, claims accrued MRWD to the wallet, and re-stakes the position in the same transaction so it keeps earning without interruption.
  • Unstake: claims rewards and withdraws the position NFT back to the wallet after confirmation.
  • Claim to Wallet: withdraws rewards already credited in the staker.

14.2 Emissions and 16-quarter schedule

  • 60% maximum reserve (600,000,000,000 MRWD). Allocated to MuttIncentiveScheduler at launch; no tokens can be minted.
  • 48-month declining linear curve across 16 quarters (MuttEmissionSchedule.sol). Monthly emissions start at 1.5× the 48-month average (18.75 billion MRWD/month) and decline linearly to 0.5× (6.25 billion MRWD/month) in month 48. The program runs as 16 consecutive quarterly periods of 91.25 days (7,884,000 seconds) starting 26 October 2026 00:07:15 UTC (1792973235): quarter k (for k = 0 … 15) holds 600,000,000,000 × (417 − 18k) / 4,512 MRWD (about 55.45 billion MRWD in Quarter 1 down to 19.55 billion MRWD in Quarter 16, summing to exactly 600 billion MRWD).
  • Quarter 1 (live): funded on UniswapV3Staker with 55.45 billion MRWD, running from 26 October 2026 00:07:15 UTC to 25 January 2027 06:07:15 UTC (1800857235).
  • Unused rewards go to 0x…dEaD. Every period's refundee is 0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD. Unclaimed rewards and any missed period are permanently removed from circulation (while remaining part of totalSupply(), as MRWD has no burn function).

14.3 Bounded authority: MuttIncentiveScheduler and automated keeper

MuttIncentiveScheduler is deployed on Base mainnet at 0xCAbf284806b800d9A771B55857B9fCF735ea7109 and verified on Sourcify (exact_match). It:

  • holds the 544.55 billion MRWD reserve for Quarters 2–16 on-chain;
  • encodes all 16 periods, the canonical pool, and the 0x…dEaD refundee immutably at construction;
  • has no owner, no admin, and no withdrawal function;
  • exposes four permissionless functions callable by any address:
    1. createIncentive(period) — creates that quarter's incentive on UniswapV3Staker inside its 30-day lead window;
    2. skipMissedPeriod(period) — if a quarter's start time passes without creation, sends that quarter's MRWD allocation to 0x…dEaD so tokens are never trapped;
    3. finalize(period, tokenIds) — after a quarter ends, unstakes any remaining positions and calls endIncentive, sending unclaimed rewards to 0x…dEaD;
    4. burnLeftover() — after all 16 quarters are settled, sends any residual balance to 0x…dEaD.

A daily GitHub Actions workflow (.github/workflows/mrwd-keeper.yml, running server/src/mrwd/keeper.ts with dedicated gas-only keeper wallet 0x5Fb98222c6010D6A3fCD3119372e82777282796f) checks the scheduler each day at 07:41 UTC and submits any due permissionless call. Quarter 2's creation window opens on 26 December 2026 06:07 UTC. Sources: [R15], [R17].

16. Measurement and economic model

The project tracks and evaluates the following metrics for the MRWD liquidity program:

Metric Definition
Depth at 1% and 2% USD trade size that moves the canonical MRWD/WETH pool's price by 1% and 2%
Reference slippage Price impact for benchmark trade sizes
LP concentration Share of active in-range liquidity held by top position owners (launch position reported separately)
In-range share Share of staked liquidity that is currently in range
Holder concentration Top-10 holder share excluding labelled custody contracts (pool, scheduler, staker, vesting, timelock, Pro staking)
Yield composition Trading-fee yield and MRWD incentive yield reported separately
Finalization delay Time between a quarter's scheduled end and its endIncentive settlement

In simplified constant-liquidity form, effective rewards claimable by staked positions are:

R_effective ≈ R_emitted × L_staked,in-range / L_total,in-range

Because rewards sold into the pool move the price, the economic model (scripts/mchow-model/) values emissions at realizable prices rather than spot valuation. Source: [R16].
