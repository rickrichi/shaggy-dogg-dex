Doggy Snacks (DSNAK)

Doggy Snacks (DSNAK, also searched in the singular as Doggy Snack) is a fixed-supply, ownerless ERC-20 ecosystem token deployed on Base Mainnet (Chain ID 8453) and paired with WETH in an active Uniswap V3 1% liquidity pool within the Shaggy Mutt DEX ecosystem.
