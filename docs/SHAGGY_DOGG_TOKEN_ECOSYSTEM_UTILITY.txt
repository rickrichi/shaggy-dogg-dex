### 1. GovernanceToken (SD)
* Contract Address: 0x051c758521971f5c5a84edaf18eea12854375469 (https://basescan.org/address/0x051c758521971f5c5a84edaf18eea12854375469)
* Total Supply: Hard-capped at 1,000,000 SD (no more can ever be minted).
* Current Balances: ~500,000 SD in your personal wallet; ~497,000 SD active in the liquidity pool.

#### Main Uses:
1. DEX Trading Asset: The native quote/base currency of the Shaggy Mutt DEX. Users buy SD with ETH, creating market demand and price appreciation.
2. Fee Discount Staking: Users who hold SD can lock it in the staking contract to reduce their trading fees and vault performance fees.
3. Governance & Voting Rights: Token holders can vote on protocol upgrades, new pool additions, fee parameters, and treasury allocations.
4. Community Incentives & Airdrops: You hold 500,000 SD in reserve that can be distributed to early liquidity providers, community members, or marketing campaigns.

### 2. Uniswap V2 LP Tokens (SD/WETH UNI-V2)
* Pair Contract Address: 0xC3D1e0b1CD05081bD7Af4cf87f681512E3E90917 (https://basescan.org/address/0xC3D1e0b1CD05081bD7Af4cf87f681512E3E90917)
* Your Holdings: 15.811388 LP tokens (100.000000% of the entire circulating supply).

#### Main Uses:
1. Automated Trading Fee Collection (Your Revenue Engine):
   * Every time anyone swaps ETH for SD or SD for ETH on Base, Uniswap V2 automatically deducts a 0.30% trading fee.
   * That fee remains inside the pool, compounding the reserves. Because you own 100% of the LP tokens, 100% of all generated fees belong to you.
2. Instant Capital Redemption:
   * At any time, you can go to the Liquidity tab, select Remove Liquidity, and burn your LP tokens to withdraw your deposited ETH and SD plus all accumulated trading fees.
3. Yield Farming & Gauge Incentives:
   * LP tokens can be staked in third-party yield farms or reward gauges to earn secondary rewards.

### 3. FeeDiscountStaking Contract
* Contract Address: 0x42134ccc6c41149cf71c7b4994e33ee66758af20 (https://basescan.org/address/0x42134ccc6c41149cf71c7b4994e33ee66758af20)

#### Main Uses:
1. Tiered Fee Discounts (Incentivizes Holding):
   * Users stake SD to unlock VIP tiers (e.g. 10%, 25%, 50%, or 100% fee discounts).
   * Creates organic demand to buy SD off the market and lock it away, reducing circulating sell pressure.
2. Anti-Dump Lock Period:
   * Features a configurable lock period (e.g., 7 days) and early exit fee (e.g., 1%) that prevents flash-loan exploitation and rewards long-term stakers.

### 4. Yield Staking Vaults (Aave v3 & Compound v3)
* Aave Vault: 0x8531807ef36560cd7db230c95db85791aa586dda (https://basescan.org/address/0x8531807ef36560cd7db230c95db85791aa586dda)
* Compound Vault: 0x38cccafb4ae96a8ef187f07fb17d18eb63e43782 (https://basescan.org/address/0x38cccafb4ae96a8ef187f07fb17d18eb63e43782)

#### Main Uses:
1. CeDeFi Yield Aggregation:
   * Users deposit stablecoins (native Base USDC 0x833589...), which are supplied to Aave v3 or Compound Comet to earn institutional lending interest.
2. Protocol Treasury Streaming:
   * Collects a configurable performance fee (default 10%, up to 30%) on generated interest, streaming passive revenue directly into your admin treasury wallet.
3. Synergy with SD Staking:
   * Vault depositors who stake SD in FeeDiscountStaking receive fee reductions on their yield, connecting your token directly to institutional DeFi lending.

### 5. AaveFlashBorrower (Uncollateralized Flash Loans)
* Contract Address: 0xa5b50037a8d2fda22805f750252268adaeb36480 (https://basescan.org/address/0xa5b50037a8d2fda22805f750252268adaeb36480)

#### Main Uses:
1. Zero-Capital Arbitrage:
   * Borrows uncollateralized capital (e.g., $100,000+ in WETH or USDC) directly from Aave v3 within a single block.
   * If SD trades at a different price on your Uniswap pool vs. Aerodrome or Uniswap V3, this contract executes triangular arbitrage, repays Aave, and keeps the net profit in your wallet.
2. Pool Rebalancing & MEV Defense:
   * Can be triggered to rebalance pool reserves or liquidate underwater positions across Base.
