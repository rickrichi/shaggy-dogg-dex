## 1. Viability criteria

A configuration is **viable** only if all hold: (1) a $1,000 trade has ≤ 5.0% execution impact both ways at launch and in every program month; (2) independent LP value ≥ 25.0% by month 3; (3) price at the program's end ≥ -50.0% of launch; (4) outside LP capital ≥ $1,000 at the end, and ≥ 50.0% of it still present 6 months after rewards stop. Criteria 3 and 4 are new in v0.3 and are proposals for approval.

## 4. Findings (with the assumptions above; re-read the tables if they change)

1. **R3 is the strongest design tested**: the protocol position in a ¼×–4× range plus the community allocation folded into rewards. It roughly halves the WETH needed for the depth gate (conservative ≈ $11,700 instead of ≈ $38,800; base and strong ≈ $9,500 instead of $19,000) and, in the conservative scenario, turns a −76% end price into about −34%, which passes the price criterion.
2. **Selling pressure is largely fixed by folding the community allocation into rewards.** With R3, the price criterion passes even with slight net selling in the conservative and base scenarios.
3. **What still fails is LP economics, and it comes down to one number: trading volume.** Without rewards, outside LPs stay only if fees pay their hurdle. Under R3 that needs about $141,000/month of volume in the base scenario (assumed $100,000), about $221,000/month in the conservative scenario (assumed $32,500), and about $379,000/month in the strong scenario (assumed $325,000).
4. **The outside-share gate is reachable in principle.** R3 needs about $7,800 of outside LP capital in the conservative scenario (≈ 6 LPs at $1,500, inside the 5–10 assumed), but LPs only arrive if returns meet their hurdle, which again depends on volume.
5. **Range risk.** A ¼×–4× protocol position stops contributing depth if the price leaves the range: above 4× it holds only WETH, below ¼× only MCHOW. In the strong scenario that happened for most of the program; depth then relied on outside LPs.
6. **The open demand question now has a target:** roughly $150,000–$220,000 of real monthly trading volume through the MCHOW pool, sustained after rewards decline. Whatever MCHOW is used for has to produce that.

## 5. Designs

- **ref**: v0.2 A48 reference: full range, community 15% released.
- **R1**: R1: protocol range ¼×–4×, community 15% released.
- **R2**: R2: full range, community folded into rewards (60%).
- **R3**: R3: protocol range ¼×–4× and community folded into rewards (60%).

- A ¼×–4× protocol range has about 2× the depth of full range per dollar while the price stays inside it; below ¼× of the launch price (a 75% fall) it holds only MCHOW and adds no depth. Out-of-range months count that.
- Folding the community allocation into rewards means it reaches only liquidity providers, over the same 48-month schedule, and no community tokens are released for sale.
- All other assumptions are as in v0.2 §7.
