## 3. Assumptions

| Scenario | Month-1 volume | Volume growth/mo | Net organic demand/mo | LP hurdle APY | Max community LP | Rewards sold | Unlocks sold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| low | $10,000 | -3.0% | $0.00 | 60.0% | $10,000 | 90.0% | 70.0% |
| baseline | $60,000 | 1.0% | $1,500 | 35.0% | $50,000 | 70.0% | 50.0% |
| high | $300,000 | 3.0% | $8,000 | 25.0% | $250,000 | 50.0% | 30.0% |
| stress | $15,000 | -6.0% | $-2.00e+3 | 80.0% | $8,000 | 100.0% | 100.0% |

- Community positions: ±50.0% ranges (capital efficiency 5.45× full range), in range 75.0% of the time, re-centred monthly; LP capital closes 50.0% of the gap to its equilibrium each month, starting from $500.
- Equilibrium LP capital: the most capital (up to the scenario cap) whose fees plus realisable rewards still meet the hurdle APY. Rewards are valued at spot less half the selling share, a crude allowance for selling into the pool.
- Emissions front-weighted, falling linearly from 1.5× to 0.5× the uniform rate; all 450B scheduled. Team 50B unlocks over months 13–48; community 150B released evenly over 24 months; treasury and any unpaired launch allocation are not sold.
- Reward sharing uses in-range liquidity: the locked full-range launch position counts against community stakers but never claims (§14.1 of the whitepaper). Finalization delay is not modelled.
- Fee tiers: the 1.00% pool is assumed to keep 50.0% of the volume a 0.30% pool would get. This single assumption decides the fee-tier comparison.
- Not modelled: arbitrage against other pools, MEV, gas, LP cost of re-centring ranges, price feedback on demand, treasury actions. Range breaches count months whose price move exceeded the community range (depth there is overstated).
