### How I'd actually configure the scanner

Do not trigger simply because:

\[
\frac{P_{sell}-P_{buy}}{P_{buy}}>T
\]

Use executable VWAP on the desired notional:

\[
E_{\text{net}}
=
\frac{VWAP_{\text{sell}}-VWAP_{\text{buy}}}
{VWAP_{\text{buy}}}
-F_{\text{buy}}
-F_{\text{sell}}
-C_{\text{hedge}}
-C_{\text{transfer}}
-C_{\text{borrow}}
\]

and require:

\[
E_{\text{net}} \ge T_i + B_{\text{latency}}+B_{\text{inventory}}
\]

For a pre-funded cross-CEX strategy, my operational thresholds would be:

- 0.08–0.12%: BTC/ETH/stablecoin markets with deep books.
- 0.15–0.20%: SOL/XRP/ADA/LINK/AVAX and similarly liquid majors.
- 0.20–0.30%: liquid large/mid-cap alts.
- 0.30–0.40%: thinner alts and high-volatility names.
- ≥0.40–0.50%: long-tail / discontinuous-liquidity markets.

These should be net thresholds. If you're screening *gross* order-book spreads, add both trading fees, expected VWAP slippage and a latency buffer first.

One reason to keep those thresholds pair-specific is visible in today's books: Binance's highest-liquidity markets are showing quoted spreads around 0.01% on BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT and USDC/USDT, while the exchange directories show materially wider spreads as you move down the liquidity curve. [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/exchanges/binance?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Also, don't build the system around transfer-after-signal arbitrage. The production architecture should keep inventory pre-positioned across venues and execute buy/sell legs near-simultaneously; blockchain transfer is primarily for subsequent inventory rebalancing.

\* `MKR` deserves special treatment because of the Maker/Sky migration; don't hard-code the legacy ticker without checking the live instrument identifier on each venue before order submission.
