### Mode A: 🤖 AI Auto-Pilot (Hands-Free Opportunity Hunter)
Best for scanning Base Mainnet blocks continuously in the background and executing qualifying spreads.

  1. Click the 🤖 AI Auto-Pilot tab (or navigate directly to /arbitrage/autopilot http://localhost:5173/arbitrage/autopilot).
  2. Configure Your Guardrails:
      • Min Net Profit Target (USD): Set your threshold (e.g., $1.00 or $5.00). The bot will disqualify anything below this net profit.
      • Max Slippage Tolerance: Default is 0.50% (50 bps). This prevents sandwich and front-running bots on Base.
      • Max Base Gas Ceiling: Set to 0.05 Gwei so transactions are only broadcast when network gas is low.
  3. Select Active Strategies:
      • ✅ Triangular Arbitrage (Cyclic WETH ↔ USDC ↔ DAI)
      • ✅ Cross-DEX Spread (Uniswap V2 ↔ Aerodrome)
      • ✅ Aave v3 Flash Borrow ($0 Collateral)
  4. Choose Execution Behavior:
      • Review First: Leave Auto-Submit to Wallet OFF. The live terminal will stream qualified opportunities with a one-click manual execute prompt.
      • Instant Execution: Toggle Auto-Submit to Wallet ON. When an opportunity meets all your criteria, it immediately prompts your wallet to sign.
  5. Click ▶ START AI AUTO-PILOT.
  6. The terminal will begin streaming block-by-block evaluations, showing detected spreads, confidence scores, and
  net profit projections.

---

Initial setup involves accessing the AI Auto-Pilot interface via the specified URL. Necessary configurations require setting a minimum net profit target, a maximum slippage tolerance (defaulting to 0.50%), and a maximum base gas ceiling. These guardrails aim to control bot behavior, preventing unprofitable trades and mitigating risks on the Bas network.

As the AI Auto-Pilot runs, the terminal streams real-time block evaluations. If Auto-Submit to Wallet is ON and a trade opportunity meets the configured criteria, the browser wallet will automatically prompt for confirmation. Upon wallet confirmation, the terminal displays the submission of the transaction and updates the profit captured display.

  When an opportunity clears all your Anti-Loss filters (Net Profit Target, Gas Ceiling, and Slippage):

  4. Track Your Net Yield:
      • Look at the top metric cards:
          • Profit Captured: Increments by your net earnings (e.g. +$4.20 USD).
          • Trades Dispatched: Increments by 1.
          • Anti-Loss Guard: Stays locked at 0.0% Loss.
      • Click the transaction link to view the receipt and token transfers directly on BaseScan https://basescan.org.
