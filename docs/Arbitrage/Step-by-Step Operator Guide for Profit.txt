Step-by-Step Operator Guide for Profit

  #### Step 1: Connect Your Wallet & Switch to Base Mainnet

  1. Open http://localhost:5173/arbitrage/matrix http://localhost:5173/arbitrage/matrix (or click Arbitrage in the
  top navbar).
  2. Click Connect Wallet in the top right corner.
  3. If prompted, ensure your network is set to Base Mainnet (Chain ID 8453).
  (Base L2 gas is ultra-cheap: typically ~$0.002 to $0.01 per transaction).
  ──────
  #### Step 2: Choose Your Execution Mode
  ──────
  ### Mode A: 🤖 AI Auto-Pilot (Hands-Free Opportunity Hunter)

  Best for scanning Base Mainnet blocks continuously in the background and executing qualifying spreads.

  1. Click the 🤖 AI Auto-Pilot tab (or navigate directly to /arbitrage/autopilot
  http://localhost:5173/arbitrage/autopilot).
  2. Configure Your Guardrails:
      • Min Net Profit Target (USD): Set your threshold (e.g., $1.00 or $5.00). The bot will disqualify anything
      below this net profit.
      • Max Slippage Tolerance: Default is 0.50% (50 bps). This prevents sandwich and front-running bots on Base.
      • Max Base Gas Ceiling: Set to 0.05 Gwei so transactions are only broadcast when network gas is low.
  3. Select Active Strategies:
      • ✅ Triangular Arbitrage (Cyclic WETH ↔ USDC ↔ DAI)
      • ✅ Cross-DEX Spread (Uniswap V2 ↔ Aerodrome)
      • ✅ Aave v3 Flash Borrow ($0 Collateral)
  4. Choose Execution Behavior:
      • Review First: Leave Auto-Submit to Wallet OFF. The live terminal will stream qualified opportunities with a
      one-click manual execute prompt.
      • Instant Execution: Toggle Auto-Submit to Wallet ON. When an opportunity meets all your criteria, it
      immediately prompts your wallet to sign.
  5. Click ▶ START AI AUTO-PILOT.
  6. The terminal will begin streaming block-by-block evaluations, showing detected spreads, confidence scores, and
  net profit projections.
  ──────
  ### Mode B: ⚡ Aave v3 Flash Loans ($0 Collateral Arbitrage)

  Best for leveraging large amounts of capital ($1,000 to $50,000) without risking your own principal.

  1. Click the 3. Flash Loans tab (or go to /arbitrage/flash-loans http://localhost:5173/arbitrage/flash-loans).
  2. Select Asset: Choose USDC or WETH.
  3. Set Borrow Amount: Enter a notional amount (e.g. 5,000 USDC).
  4. Inspect Economics:
      • The card calculates:
          • Gross Spread
          • Aave Flash Loan Fee (0.05% / 5 bps)
          • Base L2 Gas Cost (~$0.01)
          • Projected Net Profit

  5. If net profit is positive (green), click ⚡ Execute Live Flash Loan (Aave v3).
  6. Your wallet will prompt you to call your deployed AaveFlashBorrower contract on Base. The loan is borrowed,
  cycled through the spread, repaid to Aave, and the net profit is transferred to your wallet in the same transaction.
  If the profit isn't realized, the transaction reverts.
  ──────
  ### Mode C: 📐 Triangular Arbitrage (Cyclic Pool Arbitrage)

  Best for compounding native wallet capital (ETH or USDC) through multi-token liquidity pools.

  1. Click the 1. Triangular tab (or go to /arbitrage/triangular http://localhost:5173/arbitrage/triangular).
  2. Enter your Initial Capital (e.g. 0.01 or 0.05 ETH).
  3. The Quant AI Engine evaluates the 3-leg cycle:

    WETH ⟶ USDC ⟶ DAI ⟶ WETH

  4. If the cyclic product exceeds the 30 bps pool fee plus gas, the Est. Net Profit turns green.
  5. Set your slippage tolerance (recommended: 0.50% / 50 bps).
  6. Click ⚡ Execute Live Triangular Arbitrage.
  ──────
  ### Mode D: 📊 100-Pair Matrix (Market Intelligence)

  Best for identifying which tokens have the highest exchange price discrepancies.

  1. Click 📊 100-Pair Matrix (or go to /arbitrage/matrix http://localhost:5173/arbitrage/matrix).
  2. Click the quick filter 🟢 My Funded Pairs to immediately see:
      • #50 ETH/USDC (0.10% min edge)
      • #2 ETH/USDT (0.10% min edge)
      • #31 SEI/USDT (0.22% min edge)
      • #5 USDC/USDT (0.08% min edge)
  3. Click Load Pair on any row to open the live L2 depth scanner and see real bid/ask spreads across Kraken,
  Coinbase, OKX, and Uniswap.
  4. When finished inspecting, click the green [📊 Return to 100-Pair Matrix] button at the top to inspect the next
  pair.
  ──────
  ### Recommended First Move

  1. Connect your wallet to Base Mainnet.
  2. Go to 🤖 AI Auto-Pilot (http://localhost:5173/arbitrage/autopilot).
  3. Set Min Net Profit Target to $1.00.
  4. Leave Auto-Submit to Wallet OFF initially so you can observe the opportunities streaming live in the terminal
  before signing any transactions.
